## Hey, I'm Sadık 👋

I'm a 3rd-year Computer Engineering student at Selçuk University, and I spend most of my time building AI-powered applications — the kind that actually ship and compete.

My work spans a lot of ground: I've built a speech therapy app for toddlers with 3D mouth models and real-time voice analysis, fine-tuned multimodal LLMs for phishing detection, architected an autonomous FPV drone that hunts targets under GPS jamming, and developed a zero-knowledge identity protocol on Polygon. I lead AYZEK, our university's AI & algorithms community, and I work full-time as a developer at an EdTech startup while studying.

I care deeply about projects that solve real problems for real people — not just technical showpieces.

- 🏆 **1st place at ÜNİFEST 2026** (75,000 ₺) with ART-I, a speech therapy assistant for ages 2–6
- 🚀 **Teknofest multi-finalist** across Psychology Tech and Assistive Living categories
- 🔬 **TÜBİTAK-funded researcher** working on multimodal phishing detection (Qwen2-VL-7B + LoRA)
- ✈️ **Defense industry intern** at Lentatek Aerospace, 2025
- 💼 **Full Stack Developer** at Okulbox Technologies (Vuulpi project, ongoing)
- 🧠 **President** of AYZEK — Selçuk University's AI & Algorithms Community

## 🚀 What I've Built

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🗣️ ART-I — Speech Therapy Assistant
`🥇 ÜNİFEST 2026 · Teknofest 11th · Teknokent 1st & 3rd`

A mobile rehab platform for children aged 2–6 with articulation disorders. Real-time voice evaluation detects speech errors; Blender-built 3D anatomical mouth models provide visual guidance. Therapist-assisted sessions deliver personalized care without the need for a clinic visit.

`Python` `OpenCV` `React Native` `Blender` `TensorFlow`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### ✈️ AYZEK Acceltra — Autonomous Hunter Drone
`Teknofest 2026 Combat UAV · Team Captain`

Fully autonomous FPV drone that hunts and engages fixed-wing UAV targets under GNSS jamming. All compute runs ground-side: YOLOv8n detection → Kalman tracking → PID guidance → MAVLink commands. End-to-end latency: 65–70ms. Analog video pipeline with CLAHE preprocessing handles RF noise.

`Python` `YOLOv8n` `OpenCV` `Kalman Filter` `MAVLink` `CUDA`

</td>
</tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🧠 Mentorium — Agent-Based AI Tutor
`Teknofest 2026 Semi-Finalist · Team Captain`

An AI learning assistant for middle schoolers. Multi-agent architecture handles question solving, visual math comprehension, and adaptive study plan generation simultaneously. Built the entire AI module stack — from chatbot to vision reasoning to performance engine.

`Python` `FastAPI` `React Native` `PostgreSQL` `LLM Agents`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔐 NullID ZeroTrace — ZK Identity Protocol
`Teknofest 2026 Blockchain · TEKNOSEL 2026`

A decentralized zkKYC protocol where users prove attributes (age, citizenship, membership) without revealing any personal data. zk-SNARK proofs via Circom circuits; on-chain verification on Polygon in ~200k gas. No biometrics, no central storage — KVKK & GDPR compliant by design.

`Circom` `Solidity` `Polygon` `React Native` `Node.js` `snarkjs`

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🛡️ FraudMap — GNN Financial Crime Detection
`TÜBİTAK 2242 Research`

Anti-money laundering system using Graph Neural Networks. Hybrid GraphSAGE + GAT architecture detects smurfing, layering and structuring patterns that rule-based systems miss entirely. Real-time streaming via Apache Kafka; GNNExplainer output feeds automated SAR draft generation. MASAK-compliant.

`PyTorch Geometric` `Neo4j` `Apache Kafka` `FastAPI` `React`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔬 Multimodal Phishing Detection
`TÜBİTAK 2209-A · Funded Research`

Fine-tuned Qwen2-VL-7B with LoRA on a custom Turkish mobile phishing dataset — SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram screenshots with adversarial character manipulations (ı→i, ş→s) and visual perturbations. First multimodal phishing study targeting Turkish mobile channels.

`Python` `PyTorch` `LoRA` `Qwen2-VL-7B` `Gradio` `OpenCV`

</td>
</tr>

`Python` `DeepFace` `OpenCV` `TensorFlow` `Computer Vision`
